Ovalit 오발있

A Korean-language VALORANT match-review app for Android. A player links their Riot account through RSO and gets a weekly report that breaks their own matches down round by round — economy-round win rate, first-engagement outcomes, per-weapon performance, KAST, headshot rate, ACS and ADR.

The point is to show a player what to work on between sessions, not during a match. Players can add friends inside the app and compare the same metrics with them, one metric at a time. The app never produces a composite skill score.

Platform Android · Google Play · Korea APIs VAL-MATCH-V1, VAL-CONTENT-V1, VAL-STATUS-V1, RSO Status Mockups. Not yet built.
